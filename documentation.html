01 / Overview · Source pages 2–3

Two distinct worlds.

The grounded island resort gives way to an impossible ocean of clouds. The intended player route is arrival, interior discovery, the upstairs bath, then a short cloud-world excursion.

Grounded resort

Material & ritual

Skull Island port → lobby → omakase and dining → stairs → 2F bathtub threshold. The ground level is documented, but some spaces remain visibly in progress.

Impossible cloud ocean

Fog & drifting fish

LV_CloudWorld combines responsive cloud recycling, local drifting mist and collectable fish. The integrated build restores the cloud and fish systems.

Evidence boundary: The report marks the bathtub portal as in progress and the 60-second return as planned. The game-instance and timer Blueprint assets appear in the Content Browser, but this report alone does not verify their end-to-end runtime behaviour.
02 / Spatial design & assembly · Source pages 4–9, 12, 16–17

Evidence from the levels.

Pages 4–7

Skull Island massing

Lit overhead, front, left and right wireframes record the skull-shaped landform, two pools, terrain depth, roof assembly, central resort, port and vegetation. They are combined-level views—not isolated floor plans.

Page 8

Port arrival

Working views verify a boat landing, timber pier and resort entrance. These establish the approach from the water.

Pages 9 & 16

Cloud and visual tests

A side wireframe records cloud-cluster bounds, lights and scene actors. Lit front/left orthographic views record terrain brightness and building silhouette; cloud-material, VFX and audio comparisons require further evidence.

Pages 12 & 17

Interior and iteration

Views show the lobby, omakase counter, open dining and 2F graybox. Stairs establish vertical circulation. A pond landscape replaced the earlier garden concept; lobby and 2F still require refinement.
